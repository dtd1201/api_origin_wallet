# NIUM registered-beneficiary SGD LOCAL execution

Release decision: **NO-GO for financial execution**. The manual document-bundle admission mechanism has been removed. Its environment path/hash and `NiumSgdLocalReleaseEvidence` are no longer used. Production admission instead consumes `NiumRemittancePricing` through `NiumPayoutFinancialControls`. No fabricated NIUM evidence or deployable zero-fee policy was installed.

## Execution and financial controls

The existing UI prepares a dynamic owned customer/provider wallet/registered SGD LOCAL beneficiary, positive USD principal and truthful server-supported user-selected purpose. An explicit action obtains a fresh legacy NIUM FX lock. The registered-beneficiary payload sends the actual external beneficiary ID, `payout.sourceAmount`, USD source currency, legacy `auditId`, purpose and existing source-of-funds contract. It omits `destinationAmount` and SWIFT-only properties; NIUM performs conversion and payout. No speculative supplementary field or country-specific business rule was added. Account, KYC, supported corridor, purpose and authenticated registration checks remain in place.

Financial admission requires a verified adapter result matching current customer, provider environment/client/customer/wallet/account, external beneficiary, pair, rail, principal and purpose. It requires a pricing version, absolute future expiry, complete charge coverage and verified source-wallet debit allocation. Monetary inputs must be nonnegative exact decimal strings within wallet precision, fee currency must match funding currency, and all-in debit must equal principal plus confirmed source-wallet fees. Under the current principal-only CUG business policy, confirmed source-wallet fees must be zero and confirmed total debit must equal the source principal. Nonzero source-wallet charges block a new review; the application does not silently expand its reservation. This is an internal adapter contract, not an invented NIUM response schema. Unknown charges, missing allocation and incompatible currencies fail closed. Expected USD zero remains a business expectation.

The maker explicitly reviews confirmed fees and total debit. The API requires the displayed financial-terms token and rejects changed terms. Available balance must cover the all-in debit. The maker review persists principal/beneficiary/purpose/fresh audit/rate/expiry and financial authorization snapshots; an independent permitted checker revalidates the same snapshots. A changed pricing version or expiry invalidates approval. A nonzero source-wallet fee is incompatible with the principal-only funding policy and blocks approval. The UI separately labels internal reservation and expected principal debit as business policy; provider fee and total debit remain unavailable without authenticated confirmation. Exact all-in funds are reserved atomically for the durable operation, checked again before dispatch, and settled or released from the original owned reservation.

The existing nonredirecting, single-use NIUM Remittance POST, request UUID and correlation capture are preserved. Timeouts, redirects and missing references cannot trigger automatic repost or reservation release. Captured FX/payment responses can repair missing local attachment/reference without network I/O. Known references use existing status reconciliation; genuinely uncaptured outcomes require authoritative provider resolution and retain funds. Expired approved FX requires the existing explicit reset/new lock/new maker review/checker approval flow before dispatch. USD/SWIFT remains on its existing branch and does not invoke SGD financial admission.

## Reusable provider configuration

`NiumConfiguredRemittancePricing` can inspect a captured authenticated Fee Details v2 response for the current provider/client/environment. Reading presentation, requesting approval and reserving funds perform no pricing I/O. The owned, throttled `sgd-local-refresh-pricing` UI action explicitly issues only `GET /api/v2/client/{clientHashId}/fees`; it cannot acquire FX or dispatch a payment. Pricing refresh is prohibited inside a database transaction and after review/submission. No provider call was made during implementation or validation.

The local verifier checks response age (default 300 seconds, supported range 1–3600), HTTP method/status, client/provider/origin/path, response capture version, encrypted raw response and its SHA. It checks the saved v2 plan-list shape (`default`, `segment`, `status`, `fees`) without interpreting unknown fields or guessing effective pricing. Endpoint configuration is restricted to that existing versioned read contract. A failed/latest-invalid capture cannot fall back to an older success. Returned configuration references stay internal.

**Exact unresolved condition:** the available evidence and Fee Details v2 contract do not establish the customer's effective segment/override assignment, all applicable source-wallet LOCAL/FX/other charges, or their debit allocation. The existing status reconciliation settles the originally authorized reservation and does not establish an unpriced additional debit. A payment status alone cannot reconcile such an amount. Final beneficiary credit may remain an estimate; an exact guaranteed recipient amount is not an admission prerequisite. A default plan, zero `REMIT_BANK_FEE` row, stored zero or absent fee is insufficient. Therefore the production adapter returns unavailable even for a valid captured zero-fee client plan, with reason `effective_customer_pricing_and_total_debit_unverified`. Existing authenticated account, registration and FX evidence establishes their own bindings, not these financial terms. SourceAmount, omitted destinationAmount and legacy auditId confirmations remain resolved.

To permit execution, the adapter must be completed against authentic applicable provider pricing/assignment and debit-allocation semantics using a verified response/configuration contract. This does not require a hand-built document bundle. No documented provider endpoint or complete semantics supporting those missing facts exists in the available workspace evidence; none has been invented. Real pricing verification and normal release acceptance remain necessary. No commit/push/deploy commands are offered for this NO-GO candidate.

## Principal-only business policy readiness review

The application records `business_policy` with basis `internal_business_policy`, dynamic source currency/principal, reservation amount equal to principal, expected principal debit equal to principal and expected fee zero. The preparation snapshot and read-only API projection keep it separate from authenticated financial terms. The approved financial authorization binds the same policy. Customer metadata cannot promote it to provider-confirmed evidence. No funds are reserved before financial admission and independent approval; when admitted under the verified zero-fee policy, the existing owned reservation equals sourceAmount exactly. Already-dispatched reservations continue through their original settlement/release path unchanged.

Execution remains **NO-GO**: there is no authenticated customer-specific pricing/debit allocation establishing that NIUM cannot charge more than the principal to this source wallet, and no verified transaction-level charge reconciliation contract to safely account for a difference. Principal-only policy does not supply those facts. The smallest release prerequisite is verified applicable provider terms showing total source-wallet debit equals principal (including zero additional source-wallet charges), mapped by the existing pricing adapter. The client-level Fee Details v2 response alone does not establish effective customer assignment or complete debit allocation. Do not deploy for execution or enable the gate from the internal policy. No new architecture, provider requirements, document bundle or endpoint was introduced in this review.

Current review logs and exact file/preservation manifests are under `storage/framework/testing/principal-policy-readiness/`. The preceding validation counts describe the prior remittance fix; current readiness counts are recorded with these new logs.
